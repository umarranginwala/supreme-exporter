Roasted Chicory Extract (Liquid Chicory)

Concentrated liquid from expertly roasted roots—coffee-like flavour, caffeine-free.

Roasted chicory extract—also known as liquid chicory—is manufactured through advanced extraction and concentration techniques. Made from expertly roasted roots, it delivers a robust, coffee-like flavour and natural sweetness without caffeine. Used in instant coffee blends, health beverages, and flavour formulations. High solids, stable viscosity, and excellent solubility support consistent industrial performance. Packaged safely for B2B applications and preferred by global beverage brands. Controlled hot-water extraction produces a concentrated, stable liquid form.
