Dried Chicory Roots

Cleaned, sliced and naturally dried roots with consistent size, low moisture and natural aroma.

Dried chicory roots are sourced from farm-linked cultivation and carefully cleaned, sliced, and dried under controlled conditions to preserve purity and inulin content. They serve as a premium raw material for coffee substitutes, health beverages, and extract production. Consistent size, low moisture, and natural aroma support superior processing results for manufacturers seeking dependable, traceable, export-grade chicory ingredients.
