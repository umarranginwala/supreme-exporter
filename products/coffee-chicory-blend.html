Coffee–Chicory Blend

Ready industrial and retail-ready mixes—common ratios like 70:30 and 60:40, custom on request.

Coffee–chicory blend combines roasted coffee with roasted chicory powder in fixed ratios so traders and brands can buy a finished mix instead of blending in-house. Popular export ratios include 70:30 and 60:40 (coffee:chicory), with custom shares available for regional tastes—from South Indian filter styles to Middle East and European chicory coffee. Uniform grind and roast colour keep cup profile consistent container to container. Private-label packing available for qualified buyers.
