Unroasted Chicory Flour

Raw, unroasted flour rich in natural inulin for nutra, functional food and prebiotic lines.

Unroasted chicory flour is milled from dried chicory roots without roasting, preserving natural inulin—the prebiotic fibre that makes chicory valuable beyond coffee blending. It suits dietary supplements, gut-health products, functional bakery mixes, and pet nutrition where fibre enrichment matters more than roasted coffee flavour. Light colour and mild taste make it easier to formulate than dark roasted powders.
