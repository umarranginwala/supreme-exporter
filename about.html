Supreme Exporter

A Gujarat export house for chicory, dehydrates and spices.

Our story

Root to container, with export discipline.

Supreme Exporter focuses on three Gujarat-strong verticals: chicory & coffee ingredients, dehydrated onion & garlic from the Mahuva belt, and cumin-led spices from Unjha/Mehsana. We work from Ahmedabad for overseas buyers.

Every shipment is specified for moisture, roast colour and pack integrity so importers can repeat orders without reformulating.
