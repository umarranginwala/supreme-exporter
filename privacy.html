We keep business enquiry records for as long as needed for commercial follow-up and applicable record-keeping laws. We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect data; no method of transmission is fully secure.

6. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may request access, correction, or deletion of personal data we hold, or object to certain processing. Contact us at the email above. If you are in the EEA/UK, you may also contact your local data protection authority.
