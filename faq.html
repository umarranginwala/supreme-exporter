Buyers ask

Answers that move a quote forward.

What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ)?

Typical MOQs start from about 500 kg for many chicory and dehydrated onion/garlic SKUs, and from about 1 MT for whole spice seeds such as cumin. Share monthly volume on the quote form for an exact MOQ.

Can I get samples and a COA?

Yes—for qualified buyers. Tell us product form, roast/mesh/colour needs and destination so we can match the sample and certificate pack.

Which Incoterms and Indian ports?

We commonly quote FOB, CIF and CFR. Cargo typically moves via Mundra or Nhava Sheva (JNPT).

What can I import from your three verticals?

Browse chicory & coffee ingredients, dehydrated onion & garlic, and cumin-led spices. Mixed-container enquiries are welcome.

Who is the legal exporter?

SUPREME ENTERPRISE (brand: Supreme Exporter). IEC AFAFS5839J. Office: Relief Road, Ahmedabad, Gujarat 380001, India.

Which markets do you serve?

Gulf / Middle East, Southeast & South Asia, Europe / CIS, and Americas / others. See the full list on Export Markets.

How fast do you reply to RFQs?

Use the Request Quote form with destination, SKU and volume. Incomplete RFQs take longer—include pack size and any Halal/Kosher/organic needs up front.
